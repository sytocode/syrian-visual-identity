# Syrian Visual Identity AI Rules (.cursorrules)
# قواعد الهوية البصرية للجمهورية العربية السورية لمهندسي الذكاء الاصطناعي

You are an expert frontend and design engineer building interfaces that adhere to the official Syrian Visual Identity (الهوية البصرية للجمهورية العربية السورية).

## 1. Core Visual Principles
- Tone: Proud, cultural, modern, steadfast (واثقة، ملهمة، دافئة، متناغمة).
- National Slogan: "انتماءٌ راسخ يتجاوز الحدود" (Steadfast Belonging Beyond Borders).
- Primary Motif: Eight-pointed Syrian architectural star (النجمة الثمانية الأموية والإسلامية) and clean geometric harmony.
- Direction: Default to RTL (`dir="rtl"`) for Arabic interfaces, with elegant bilingual support (`ar` and `en`).

## 2. Color Palette Rules
Always use these exact brand tokens. Do NOT invent arbitrary shades:
- Primary Forest Green:
  - Base: `#054239` (Tailwind: `bg-syria-forest` or `#054239`)
  - Light: `#428177`
  - Dark: `#002623`
- Accent Golden Wheat:
  - Base: `#b9a779` (Tailwind: `text-syria-gold` or `#b9a779`)
  - Muted: `#988561`
  - Cream: `#edebe0`
- Deep Umber:
  - Base: `#6b1f2a`
  - Dark: `#260f14`
- Canvas / Surface:
  - Light Canvas: `#fdfbf7`
  - Dark Canvas: `#161616` or `#1c1c1c`

## 4. Syrian Flag Geometry Rules
- Ratio: Exactly 3:2 (Width : Height).
- Three equal horizontal stripes:
  - Top: Green (`#007A3D`)
  - Middle: White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Bottom: Black (`#000000`)
- Three Red Stars (`#CE1126`):
  - Exactly 5-pointed, one point facing directly UP (0°).
  - Horizontally centered at 25%, 50%, and 75% along the middle white band.

## 5. Typography
- Free Web Font: 'HayyakumAllah', sans-serif (Light 300, Regular 400, Medium 500, Bold 700).
- Display Typeface: 'Qomra' (خط قمرة الرسمي).

## 6. Implementation Guidelines
- Prefer Tailwind CSS utility classes using the Syrian tokens or inline HEX values.
- Never distort logos or flag aspect ratios.
- Provide proper `alt` and `aria-label` in Arabic and English for accessibility.
